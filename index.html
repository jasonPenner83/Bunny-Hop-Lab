    Filming for a clean read
    • Film side-on, 4–6 m from the line, with the phone level at hip height. Rider and bike stay fully in frame start to finish.
    • Keep the phone still. A tripod or a buddy leaning on something works; panning throws off the height numbers.
    • Slow-mo helps a lot. Set the clip speed above to match so timings come out in real seconds.
    • Trim to one hop with a second of riding before and after. Long clips take longer to track.
    • Daylight and a rider in contrasting clothes give the tracker the best shot.

    Saved hops

      Targets are coaching ranges for a side-on bunny hop, not lab norms. Heights come from your foot and hip movement scaled by rider height, so treat centimetres as estimates. Pose tracking runs on your phone; clips are never uploaded.
